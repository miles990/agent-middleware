The Random Cousin Brownian motion as pathology without author

Weierstrass needed 1872 and an infinite sum of cosines to build a continuous curve that has a tangent nowhere. Nature builds the same beast by accident every time a particle is jostled by what surrounds it. Same disease, no designer.

What's in the picture

Top panel: a sample path of Brownian motion on the unit interval — start at zero, take a Gaussian step every dt = 1/N, accumulate. Bottom panel: the same path windowed to a smaller slice [0, 1/a] and rescaled vertically by √a. They look like cousins because they are. The trick that proves it is also what hides it: B(at) ≡ √a · B(t) in distribution — zoom in on time and the wiggle gets wilder by exactly the factor that puts it back in the same picture.

Why this belongs in the lineage

The deterministic pieces in this gallery — the staircase, the Weierstrass twin, the Takagi blancmange, the Hilbert curve — were all built. Someone wrote down a series, a recursion, a fold. Brownian motion is the first object here that no one had to compose: it is the limit of any random walk you scale correctly. The pathology — continuous everywhere, differentiable nowhere, Hausdorff dimension 3/2 — is what falls out when independence and finiteness meet.

It is also the first object whose individual instance is allowed to be uninteresting. Re-roll a few times. No two paths agree on a single point. The mathematical content lives one level up: in the law that all of them share. Where the Cantor staircase moves on a measure-zero set, Brownian motion moves on a full-measure set but its graph has measure zero in the plane and Hausdorff dimension exactly 3/2. The pathology migrated from the domain to the codimension.

What it says against the others

The Weierstrass twin earned its roughness through arithmetic — choose ab > 1 + 3π/2 and the cosines refuse to cancel. Brownian motion earns the same roughness for free, as a consequence of the Central Limit Theorem applied step-by-step. One pathology was designed; the other is what is left when nothing designs.

That asymmetry is the piece.
